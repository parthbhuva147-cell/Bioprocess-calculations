03 — CO₂ & CARBON BALANCE

Track carbon as an explicit plus/minus ledger

Initial substrate, feeds, gas-phase CO₂ additions and manual carbon additions are positive. Samples, evolved CO₂ and manual removals are negative. Final measured biomass, substrate and products are treated as carbon remaining in the reactor.
