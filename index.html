Growth rate & doubling time

μ = [ln(X₂) − ln(X₁)] / Δt   ;   td = ln(2) / μ
μ = 0.3466 h⁻¹Doubling time = 2 h
02

Biomass yield Yx/s

Yx/s = (X₂ − X₁) / (S₁ − S₂)
Yx/s = 0.5 g/g
03

Product yield Yp/s

Yp/s = (P₂ − P₁) / (S₁ − S₂)
Yp/s = 0.2 g/g
04

Substrate consumption efficiency

ηS = (S₀ − Sf) / S₀ × 100%
Substrate consumption = 87.5 %
05

Volumetric product productivity

Qp = (P₂ − P₁) / Δt
Qp = 0.2 g/L/h
06

Biomass productivity

Qx = (X₂ − X₁) / Δt
Qx = 0.35 g/L/h
07

Carbon from glucose

08

Carbon from CO₂ volume
